Fitness and nutrition information

Fitness 46 provides general fitness, workout, food, calorie, and BMI information for educational purposes. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a personalised nutrition plan. Calculators use estimates; food values can vary by brand, portion, and preparation, and results may not reflect your individual needs.

Get advice for your situation

Speak with a qualified doctor, dietitian, or other appropriate professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you are under 18, pregnant, have a medical condition, take medication, or have a history of disordered eating. Stop exercising and seek appropriate help if you feel unwell or experience pain. Do not use this site for emergencies.

Exercise and use of the site

You are responsible for choosing activities that are suitable for your ability, equipment, and surroundings. Follow safe technique and equipment instructions. Your use of workouts, calculators, and other guidance is at your own discretion.

Community posts and comments are shared by members. They represent their authors and are not verified or endorsed professional advice from Fitness 46. Do not share private or sensitive health information in public community discussions.
