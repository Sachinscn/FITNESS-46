Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Information this site uses

When you use Fitness 46, we may process the information you provide for the features you choose to use:

  • Account and profile: your name, email address, age, fitness goal, and activity level.
  • Progress tracker: your starting weight, check-in weights, goal, activity level, start date, and any optional notes you save.
  • Community: posts, comments, likes, and photos that you choose to share.
  • Sign-in session: your browser stores authentication information so you can stay signed in and use your account.

How we use it

We use this information to create and maintain your account, show and save your profile and personal progress, display community activity, and respond to support requests.

Who can see information

Your profile and progress entries are private to your account. Community posts, comments, and photos are shared with signed-in community members. Please avoid posting private contact details or sensitive health information in community posts.

Service provider

Fitness 46 uses Supabase for account authentication, database storage, and community photo storage. Information needed for these features is processed by Supabase on our behalf under its service terms and privacy practices. Your sign-in session is also kept in your browser to support login.

Retention and your choices

We keep account, progress tracker, and community information for as long as needed to operate the site and handle related requests. To ask us to access, correct, or delete information associated with your account, contact us using the email below.

Security

We use access controls in the site and its backend to protect information. No website or online storage can guarantee absolute security, so use a unique password and sign out on shared devices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when site features or data practices change. The updated date at the top will show the latest revision.

Privacy questions or requests?
Email help.fitness46@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
